Timecode

Frames ↔ timecode ↔ seconds at any frame rate. Retime between rates, check slow-mo speed, measure in/out durations.

01 · Frame rate

02 · Convert

Type in any field, the other two follow. Timecode takes 01:02:03:04, 2:12 (seconds:frames) or just digits 1020304.

03 · To another rate

Same duration = moving a cut to a new timeline rate. Same frames = conforming footage (shoot 60, play at 24 → 40% slow-mo).

04 · Duration

Out is exclusive, the way Premiere, Resolve and After Effects count. If your out point is the last frame you keep, add 1 frame.
